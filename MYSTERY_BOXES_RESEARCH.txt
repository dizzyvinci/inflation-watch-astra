MARKET JOIN AND EXPECTED-VALUE METHOD
Join exact casefolded official reward names to exactly one cashCatalog provider row. Ambiguous multiple rows, absent names, metadata-only records and guessed aliases do not yield prices. The daily observation must match the selected calendar day exactly and have positive median and integer recorded count>=5; do not carry older prices into missing same-day outcomes.
Expected gross resale proxy = SUM(publishedPercent_i/100 * same-day dailyMedian_i), only when every outcome has a qualifying price. Published odds are rounded and are retained without renormalization. Missing outcomes leave EV unavailable, with count/probability coverage. No assumption of equal rates or individual transaction access. Earlier-than-edition dates cannot value that edition's rewards. Dated values after an announcement window do not establish current ability to buy/open a box or historical continuity of its rate page.
The single and bundle NX/stamp purchase prices remain separate facts. Expected gross value for11(or10) openings is that many times the single-opening proxy under the captured odds, not an observed bundle transaction or guaranteed payout. Crate-name daily unit median and N*unit-median proxy are separate source valuations; same-name units cannot be identified by edition. No daily medians are called asks, bids, realized profits or live availability. Fees, selling delays, liquidity, quality mix and unobserved trades are outside this proxy.
