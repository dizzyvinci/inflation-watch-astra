MapleStory Market’s published hourly chat/megaphone medians. Spread = seller median − buyer median; percentage spread divides that difference by their midpoint. OPEN METHODOLOGY

Show the work.

A useful market measure starts with knowing what is being measured.

A basket based on spending why 50?

Basket v2 freezes the 50 non-cash items with the largest estimated meso turnover in October 21–27, 2026: daily median price × recorded sold volume. Eligibility requires at least five reference days with recorded volume of 50 or more. For eligible items, all reference days with at least five recorded sales enter the base calculations. These counts do not establish stack quantities or independent traders.

Base price is the sale-volume-weighted average of daily medians in that reference week. Base spending is that price multiplied by recorded base sale volume. Weights are spending shares, capped at 8% with excess redistributed until all weights sum to 100%. Thus a cheap ore with many sales and an expensive scroll with fewer sales both contribute in proportion to estimated mesos spent. The cap applies to base weights; a large later price change can still have a larger effect.

This v2 history is reconstructed using a fixed basket selected later. It is separate from the earlier count-selected v1 series, whose definition and published values remain archived. An index change caused by switching definitions is not a new market move.

CPIₜ = 100 × Σ wᵢ × (priceᵢ,ₜ / base priceᵢ)wᵢ ∝ base priceᵢ × base sale volumeᵢ · max 8%

Daily medians × sold volume approximate turnover; they do not equal an exact transaction-value sum. The source does not expose the underlying stack-level trades here. There is no claim to measure total meso supply. Clean and upgraded equipment can mix in aggregate medians, introducing quality/composition bias.

Why 50 items?

Fifty is this project’s initial, inspectable market basket size, not a U.S. CPI rule or a statistical guarantee. It focuses on regularly traded items with usable reference-week observations. The coverage below shows how much activity it captures; excluded items and changes in item quality can behave differently.

Membership stays fixed between explicit basket versions. We review broader coverage and category concentration separately; adding items would create a new version and preserve this history. Recorded sale counts are not independent-trader sample sizes, and a larger count alone does not establish accuracy.

What counts as evidence?

Shop observations→Source-inferred sales→Daily medians + volume

These are scanner-derived sale estimates rather than user-entered asks. The source’s underlying scanner may infer a sale when a shop closes and an item is gone. A disappearing listing or a grey row is evidence with limitations; neither independently proves a trade. We do not classify an item as sold merely from its sprite color.

Using a fixed basket, sale-volume-weighted prices and capped spending shares makes a single posted quote less influential after this site is published. Smega asks and player reports never enter the CPI; these choices reduce manipulation risk, while coordinated trades, wash trading and scanner errors can still distort observed sales.

Asking prices, signed-in player reports, and smega advertisements are separate signals. They are labeled and plotted separately when observations are available. Missing series stay empty instead of inventing history.

How this compares with the US CPI

Shared principleWeight price changes by spending in a reference basket.
This indexVirtual items, one-week base, market medians, fixed item mix, no seasonal or quality adjustment.
US CPIHousehold expenditure weights; modified Laspeyres aggregation, geometric means in many lower-level categories, and quality adjustments.

Our cap, narrow market coverage, short base period and game-specific supply shifts make this a commodity-style market index, rather than a household cost-of-living index. BLS methodology ↗

HONEST FRESHNESS

More polls ≠ more trades.

The collector checks every 10 minutes. When upstream data changes, all calculations and charts refresh together. A daily feed remains daily data, even if checked frequently.

Intraday scanner inputs require timestamps, sold evidence and coverage thresholds. Below 80% CPI basket weight or 60% NX volume across three cash items, the corresponding headline stays on the daily snapshot.

SOURCE HEALTH / PROVENANCE

Always know how old it is.

Market observation time, source publication time, and our check time are different clocks.

What happens when something breaks?

The collector keeps the last valid feed when an upstream request fails. Malformed data and overlapping collectors cannot replace it. Every snapshot preserves its original source time. The dashboard turns amber after 40 minutes and red after three hours without fresh intraday observations; the daily snapshot is explicitly dated throughout.

Your open tab checks a small version file about once a minute while visible, with jitter and backoff. Prices are fetched once by the collector, never per visitor. Charts and tables use the same versioned payload.
